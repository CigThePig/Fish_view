Only the right-facing versions of qualifying fish are stored. Left-facing art
is generated by `src/art/mirror.js`. Fish wider than eight cells and all ducks,
boats, ships, whales, monsters, sharks, splats, and castles were intentionally
excluded from Phase 0.

## Fish View original artwork

The `twin-sail` species and every pre-adult growth stage in
`src/art/sprites.js` were drawn for Fish View rather than extracted from
`asciiquarium`, in the same eight-cell right-facing vocabulary and under the
same GPL v2-or-later terms as the rest of this repository. The `max` stage of
each species is the extracted adult sprite itself.
